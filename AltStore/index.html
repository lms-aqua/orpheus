What sideloading means here

These builds are unsigned. AltStore signs the app on your device with your own Apple ID, so no signing certificate is shared through this repository.

  • With a free Apple ID, the app stops opening after 7 days unless AltStore refreshes it.
  • With a paid Apple Developer account, it lasts 1 year.

Because entitlements are applied when an app is signed, a sideloaded ORPHEUS does not carry the app-wide Data Protection default. Encrypted content still sets its protection class explicitly, but the metadata database falls back to the system default class. This is a real difference from a properly signed build, and it is written down rather than glossed over.

Status

ORPHEUS is in development. The encryption layer is implemented and tested; storage, authentication, and the content types are not finished yet, so these builds cannot save anything.

Install them to look at the interface, not to keep anything in.
